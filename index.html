HALLO ROOM by T.Koi

Why doesn't HALLO ROOM appear inside iPad Control Center → AirPlay?
Apple restricts the native Control Center AirPlay list to hardware/native receivers (Apple TV, Mac, or installed receiver software)—web browsers cannot register as native AirPlay targets.

Fastest 1-Tap Classroom Workflow:

  1. Lock your daily room name (e.g. HALLO) on the Classroom Board.
  2. Point each student iPad camera at its slot's QR Code (iPad 1–4).
  3. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen once per iPad.
  4. Every day after that: students just tap the HALLO ROOM icon and press the giant TAP TO MIRROR SCREEN button!
