AirPlay Note — Why QR & 1-Tap Connect

HALLO ROOM by T.Koi

Why doesn't HALLO ROOM show up inside iPad Control Center → AirPlay?
Apple locks the native Control Center AirPlay receiver list to hardware Apple TVs and native macOS/Windows receiver apps—web browsers cannot broadcast Bonjour/mDNS AirPlay services.

How HALLO ROOM Delivers Faster 4-iPad Classroom Mirroring:

  1. Native AirPlay only shows 1 iPad at a time; HALLO ROOM streams 4 horizontal iPads side-by-side over direct LAN WebRTC with hardware H.264 + zero receiver buffering.
  2. Point each student iPad camera at its slot's QR Code (Slots 1–4) to jump directly to the 1-Tap screen with Room, Slot, and Latency Mode pre-filled.
  3. Tap Safari Share → Add to Home Screen once per iPad so students connect in one tap every class.
